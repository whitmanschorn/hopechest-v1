STATE OF CALIFORNIA · COUNTY OF ORANGE

LICENSE AND CERTIFICATE OF MARRIAGE

Local registration number 65-01487

## License

These presents are to authorize and license any person duly authorized by the laws of the State of California to solemnize marriage within the said County of Orange, to solemnize the marriage of

GEORGE OSCAR BLUTH, a native of California, aged 24 years, a resident of Newport Beach, County of Orange, occupation: proprietor, frozen banana stand,

and

LUCILLE JENKINS, a native of California, of full age, a resident of Newport Beach, County of Orange, occupation: waitress.

Neither party has been previously married.

In witness whereof, I have hereunto set my hand and affixed the seal of the County Clerk of said county, this 9th day of February, 1965.

W. E. Sinclair, County Clerk
By D. Pruitt, Deputy

## Certificate of person performing ceremony

I hereby certify that the above-named George Oscar Bluth and Lucille Jenkins were joined by me in marriage, in accordance with the laws of the State of California, at Newport Beach, County of Orange, on the 13th day of February, 1965.

Signed: Hon. Arthur P. Mosely, Judge of the Municipal Court, Newport Beach Judicial District

Witnesses to the ceremony:

Oscar G. Bluth, of Newport Beach

Mrs. Evelyn Sinclair, of Costa Mesa

## Registration

Received for registration and recorded in the office of the County Recorder of Orange County, California, this 17th day of February, 1965.

Book 212 of Marriages, page 318
