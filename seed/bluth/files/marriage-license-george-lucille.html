License

These presents are to authorize and license any person duly authorized by the laws of the State of California to solemnize marriage within the said County of Orange, to solemnize the marriage of

GEORGE OSCAR BLUTH, a native of California, aged 24 years, a resident of Newport Beach, County of Orange, occupation: proprietor, frozen banana stand,

and

LUCILLE JENKINS, a native of California, of full age, a resident of Newport Beach, County of Orange, occupation: waitress.

W. E. Sinclair
