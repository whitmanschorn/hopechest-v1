# Corrections

facts were checked against the wiki articles in `documents/`. Where the two disagreed, the
article wins. Where the articles are silent, the fact was removed or recorded as a deduction.

## Dates removed or made less exact

No article supports these, so they are gone or reduced to what is supported:

- G.O.B.'s birthday (was May 4, 1965). Now "around 1965", a deduction from his being older than
  Michael.
- Birth dates for Lucille (1948), Tobias (April 4, 1964) and Steve Holt (1985). Removed.
- Exact days for the arrest, Michael taking over, the banana stand fire (all November 2003), the
  seal attack (January 2005) and the Queen Mary (February 2006). These were broadcast dates of
  episodes. Now the year only.

## Facts changed

- **Lucille's maiden name** was "Lucille". It is Jenkins.
- **Lindsay's origin.** She was recorded as born Nellie Sitwell, daughter of Stan Sitwell. The
  articles disagree with each other; most say Stan Sitwell nearly adopted her and the Bluths got
  there first. Her biological mother was Mimi, which makes her Lucille's half-sister. Now: born
  "Nellie", Mimi as biological mother, George Sr. and Lucille as adoptive parents.
- **George Michael and Maeby's wedding** was recorded as non-binding. The articles say the priest
  was real and the marriage official. Now a marriage event and a partner link.
- **Annyong** was recorded as adopted by Lucille alone, with a real name the family never
  learns. He was adopted by both, and he reveals his name in 2006.
- **George Sr.'s arrest** was placed on the Queen Mary. It happened aboard the Marina Hornblower,
  now a place of its own. The charge then was fraud; "light treason" came later.
- **The Queen Mary** was "stolen" and "ran aground" during a "second retirement party". It was
  rented for George's exoneration party and capsized; Lucille was arrested for sinking it.
- **The banana stand fire** was "for the insurance", by Michael and George Michael together. No
  article mentions insurance, and they disagree on who lit it. The event now says so.
- **Buster and the Army.** He was recorded as enlisting to defy Lucille. Lucille signed him up.
  The seal attack excused him from service for a time; it did not end it.
- **Steve Holt** was recorded as learning who his father is in 2005. Only the audience learns
  then; he traces G.O.B. later. The event was dropped, and Eve Holt added as his mother.
- **Lindsay's campaign** was "against Lucille Austero". Lucille Austero ran against Herbert Love;
  Lindsay ran on Love's side and Sally Sitwell took over from Lucille Austero.
- **Tobias and Lindsay** were "divorcing". They separate and reunite; no divorce is recorded.
  Tobias was a psychoanalyst and psychotherapist, not a psychiatrist.
- **Tracey**: "two years before the documentary begins" was unsupported and removed.
- **Places**: the banana stand is on the Oceanside Wharf boardwalk; Wee Britain is in Newport
  Beach; the cabin's supposed location (Big Bear) was unsupported and removed, with its map pin.
  Other pins are kept for the map and marked approximate.

## Invented for testing

One document, with its scanned-looking picture in `files/`, is not from the wiki. `documents/marriage-license-george-lucille.md` is a fictional
Orange County marriage licence and certificate, written so that "when did grandma and grandpa get
married?" has a family paper as its source of truth, the way a real chest would. It fixes the
wedding at Newport Beach on February 13, 1965, with Oscar as a witness. Nothing in the series or
the wiki gives that date. It agrees with what the wiki does say: Lucille was a waitress, was
expecting G.O.B., and had been with Oscar first.

The marriage event cites the licence, so its date is exact and no longer a deduction. G.O.B.'s
birth stays a deduction ("around 1965").

## Known gaps

  brothers yet.
- G.O.B.'s brief marriage is not an event because his wife is never named.
- Tracey is also spelled "Tracy" in the articles; "Tracy Bluth" is kept as an alternate name.

## Caption leftovers removed from the wiki text (2026-11-12)

The copied articles carried the tail ends of picture captions, such as `"]]` or
`" in "Good Grief"]]`, at the start of paragraphs. They are markup debris, not text. The files
are left as copied; `stripCaptionLeftovers` in `scripts/lib/seedData.ts` removes the debris when
the seed is read, so documents and wiki pages are both clean. No fact changed.
